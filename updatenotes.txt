What's new:
 
Welcome to Noctra on mobile! Your favorite IPTV player is now available on Android.
 
Watch Live TV, Movies, and Series with a brand-new mobile design built for your phone. Enjoy smooth gesture controls for volume and brightness, picture-in-picture mode, and background playback.
 
Browse your My List, Favorites, and History. Download content for offline viewing. Import your own M3U playlists from your device.

Everything you love about Noctra on desktop, now in your pocket.
