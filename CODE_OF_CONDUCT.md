# Noctra Collaboration Standards

These standards apply to Kynora Studio personnel and authorized contributors
working on Noctra.

## Expected Conduct

- Communicate professionally and respectfully.
- Give technical feedback that is specific, factual, and focused on the work.
- Respect differences in experience, background, identity, and viewpoint.
- Protect confidential source code, credentials, user data, reports, and
  business information.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and third-party material before submission.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help correct them promptly.

## Unacceptable Conduct

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, or sexualized conduct.
- Publishing another person's private or confidential information.
- Sharing proprietary source code or internal material without authorization.
- Introducing malicious code, hidden access, credential collection, or license
  bypasses.
- Knowingly submitting code or assets that infringe third-party rights.
- Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the responsible Kynora Studio
maintainer or to **kynora.studio@gmail.com**. Reports will be reviewed with
appropriate confidentiality. Kynora Studio may remove access, reject work, or
take other proportionate action when these standards are violated.
