Noctra 1.2.0 — What's new

New
- Update center: Check for and install new versions right from Settings.
- Rate Noctra: After you've actually used the app, we'll ask if you'd like to leave a Store rating.
- Welcome screen: A short, clear notice about what Noctra does (and doesn't) include, shown on first launch.
- Better PIN protection: Lockout after failed attempts, weak-PIN warning, PIN reset, and screen-reader support.
- Local playlists: Add a profile from a playlist file on your PC — with validation and progress feedback.
- Easier sign-in: Show/hide password toggle and automatic MAC formatting for Stalker accounts.
- Upgraded Download Center: Sort downloads (newest, A–Z, size), see how much space your downloads use, and retry or delete failed ones.
- Right-click menus on cards: Add to Favorites or My List, remove from history, and more — right on the card.
- New pickers for categories and sorting: Cleaner selection screens instead of dropdowns.
- Clear search button in the header, plus faster and smarter search results.

Improved
- Program Guide: Program titles now appear in your preferred language, channel mapping is much more accurate (no more wrong-guide mix-ups), a single "Now" line shows your position, and large guide updates no longer stall.
- Performance: Smoother scrolling with big download queues and long episode lists; faster startup, channel loading, and image loading.
- Friendlier messages: Errors are now written in plain language — no technical jargon.
- Better-looking app: Unified buttons, input fields, and cards; theme-aware logos; refreshed Premium badges.
- Smarter Premium: Promo codes give clearer feedback, expired Premium refreshes automatically.
- Playback: Improved resume, clearer playback info, and more reliable controls after the window loses and regains focus.
- Downloads reliability: Paused, failed, and resumed downloads behave more consistently.

Removed
- Child Profile mode: We couldn't guarantee reliable content filtering, so it's gone. Existing child profiles are deleted on first launch after this update (you'll see a one-time notice).
